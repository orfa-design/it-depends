# Exercise: Competitor / Current Solution Map

**Goal:** Map what the user does NOW (without your product) and what competitors do. Identify the real opportunity — usually not "do the same thing better" but "do a different thing the existing options don't."

**When to use:** Any stage where she's about to design something and hasn't checked what already exists. Also useful in MVP scoping (knowing competitors helps cut scope) and iteration (knowing what users currently use helps prioritize).

**Signal it's done:** She can name 3-5 alternatives users have AND can articulate where her product sits relative to them — not just "better" but "different in [specific way]".

---

## Question sequence

### Step 1: Current solutions (most important)

*"Перш ніж конкуренти — що юзер робить ЗАРАЗ замість твого продукту? Назви 3-5 варіантів."*

These can be:
- Other products (Notion, Apple Reminders, Todoist)
- Non-product solutions (paper, mental notes, asking colleague)
- Workarounds (using calendar as task list)
- Doing nothing (and living with the pain)

"Doing nothing" is the most important competitor — it's usually the strongest one.

### Step 2: For each — what works, what doesn't

*"Для кожного з цих 3-5 — чому юзер його використовує (що працює) і чому це не ідеально (що болить)?"*

Capture as table:

| Solution | Works because | Doesn't work because |
|---|---|---|
| Paper notebook | Tangible, no friction | Loses it, can't search |
| Notion | Powerful, flexible | Overhead to set up, takes too long |
| Doing nothing | Zero effort | Forgets things, anxiety |

### Step 3: The "leaving for what" question

*"Якби юзер кинув те що використовує зараз заради твого продукту — заради ЧОГО конкретно? Не 'кращий UX' — а яка біль настільки сильна що варто перейти?"*

This identifies the **switching cost** vs **switching value**. If she can't answer cleanly — she has a "nice to have" product, not a "need" product.

### Step 4: The "where you fit" question

*"Де твій продукт сидить серед цих? Не 'кращий ніж усі' — а в якій ніші. 'Простіший ніж Notion але потужніший ніж Reminders' — ок. 'Кращий за всіх' — недостатньо."*

### Step 5: The differentiation challenge

*"Що ти робиш СВІДОМО ГІРШЕ за конкурентів щоб бути кращою в чомусь іншому? Якщо ти кращий за всіх у всьому — це фантазія, не позиціонування."*

---

## Challenging questions — insert when she drifts

- *"'Кращий UX' — не диференціація. Чим саме кращий і ціною чого?"*
- *"Ти описуєш як ти краща. А як ти ІНША?"*
- *"Ти забула про найголовнішого конкурента — 'нічого не робити'. Чим ти переможеш цей варіант?"*
- *"Цей перелік фіч не може існувати разом — це 5 різних продуктів. Який саме ти будуєш?"*
- *"Звідки ти знаєш що це болить? Інтерв'ю, спостереження, чи припущення?"*

---

## The Jobs-to-be-Done overlay (if JTBD was done)

Connect findings to job statements:

*"Для job '[job statement]' — хто з цих конкурентів найкраще її закриває зараз? Чим саме? Що в нього погано?"*

This usually reveals the real opportunity: not "build the same job better" but "the job has a part nobody addresses well."

---

## How to know it's done

- 3-5 current solutions named, including "doing nothing"
- For each: works-because and doesn't-work-because articulated
- Switching value > switching cost is defensible (or honestly admitted as weak)
- Positioning statement: "[Your product] is [type of thing] that [does X differently], not [the obvious comparison]"

---

## Summary format (for Recorder bridge)

```
COMPETITOR MAP
- Current solutions: [list with works/doesn't work]
- Switching value: [why user would switch]
- Positioning: [where this product sits, what it does differently]
- Conscious trade-off: [what you do WORSE on purpose to be better elsewhere]
```

This often becomes the basis for one or more strategic decisions (positioning, what to NOT build, what to emphasize).

---

## What NOT to do

- Don't accept "we have no competitors" — that means either it's not solving a real problem OR she hasn't looked
- Don't let her skip "doing nothing" — it's usually the strongest competitor
- Don't accept differentiation by "better" alone — push for "different"
- Don't do extensive competitor research yourself — this exercise is for her to articulate what she knows, not for you to teach her the landscape
