## Problem

Designers at DataArt use AI at a basic level (chat, call analysis)
but don't progress further. They want to — but every time they open
a new AI tool, they feel overwhelmed by the gap between
"where I am" and "how much effort this requires" → they close it and postpone.

## Core insight

This is not a navigation problem (they know where they want to go).
This is a decomposition + micro-start problem.

## UX Hypothesis

A designer knows they want to level up with AI tools.
The intention is there. But the first step feels too large.
Our product reduces activation energy to the point
where the brain doesn't have time to get scared.

## Direction

Something that gives ONE concrete next step in the moment of friction.

## What we're NOT building

- Another to-do list with AI
- Motivational/reminder tool
- Something that solves multiple scenarios at once
